From Liège

Stoneberized is a nu-metal band from Liège, Belgium, formed in 2022 and fed as much by stoner rock as by alternative metal.

Their sound swings between weight and tension — massive riffs, thick textures and more frontal passages, carried by hybrid vocals somewhere between rap and scream. No walls between genres: the influences cross freely.

After a first demo in 2023, aired on Classic 21, the band is back with Wage Effect, a 4-track EP built for the stage.
